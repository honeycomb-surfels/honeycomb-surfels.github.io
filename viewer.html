Open your own model

Open a Hexels deployment archive (.hexcodec) or a viewer bundle (.hexview, .hexview.gz) from your computer, or drop it anywhere on this page. The file never leaves your device: the browser reads, unpacks and renders it locally, and nothing is uploaded.

Make a .hexcodec from your own run

The .hexcodec archives are the deployment files of the Hexels code, the same files the paper's tables evaluate. For a V3 model of your own, run from the Hexels repository root, on any computer (no GPU needed):

PYTHONPATH=. python -m hexels3dgs.compact_checkpoint \
    --checkpoint <run>/model/checkpoints/ckpt_030000.pt \
    --output deployment.pt --active_only
PYTHONPATH=. python -m scripts.hexels_sh_codec pilot \
    --checkpoint deployment.pt --config <run>/model/config.json \
    --output codec --clusters 1024

Then open codec/sh_pq1024.hexcodec (quantized) or codec/lossless.hexcodec here. Any V1, V2 or V3 checkpoint also opens as a viewer bundle, which carries the training cameras as saved views when the COLMAP scene is available:

PYTHONPATH=. python -m hexels3dgs.viewer_model \
    <run>/model/checkpoints/ckpt_030000.pt scene.hexview

The browser cannot open training checkpoints (.pt) themselves.

The viewer's Controls button opens display modes (primitive IDs, hexagon boundaries, depth, normals, opacity, a side-by-side comparison), the training views, quality settings and screenshots. On a phone, Full window gives the most room.

About these models

    • Each scene opens at one of its training views. Like any reconstruction from photos, it looks best near the captured views; far from them, holes and blurry floaters appear.
    • The downloads are the compressed deployment archives of the trained models (.hexcodec). Your browser unpacks them before rendering, so a model needs more memory while it opens than its download size suggests.
    • PSNR is the mean over each scene's held-out test views (every eighth image), measured by the CUDA renderer on the decoded archive that you download here.
    • The browser renderer evaluates the same hexagon footprint and color model in WebGL2. It matches the CUDA renderer closely, not bit for bit: depending on the device it blends in 32-bit or 16-bit floats.
    • Everything runs on your device: after the download, moving the camera sends nothing anywhere. Full quality needs a recent laptop or desktop GPU and enough memory; on phones and tablets, start with Compact. If motion stutters, choose Adaptive preview under the viewer's Controls.
